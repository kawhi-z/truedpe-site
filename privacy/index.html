Effective date: November 24, 2026

Who we are: Xinfeng Rongchuang Technology Information Consulting Service Department (Individually Owned Business), Xinfeng County, Ganzhou, China ("we", "us"). Contact: zhangdagen@xfrongchuang.com.

TrueDPE is a practice app for the FAA Private Pilot oral exam. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and how to delete it. We wrote it to match how the app actually works.

The short version

  • You sign in with Apple. We keep only an anonymous ID from Apple — no name, no email.
  • Your voice never leaves your phone. Speech is transcribed on the device; only the text is sent.
  • Your answers (text) are sent to an AI provider to grade them and to generate the examiner's next question.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or use third-party analytics or tracking. We count how the app is used with our own analytics, which you can turn off in Settings. We count how the app is used with our own analytics service, and you can turn that off in Settings.
  • You can delete your account and all your data from inside the app at any time.

How we use your data to improve the app

When you dispute a grade, or tell us how your real checkride went, we may review it (with the related question and answer) to check and improve how the app grades and what it asks. These records are tied to your anonymous account ID, not to your name. They are deleted when you delete your account.

How long we keep it

  • While your account exists, we keep your records so your history, debriefs and review plan keep working.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your data from our live database immediately.
  • Backups are kept on our server for 7 days and then deleted. If we keep an off-site copy of a backup, it is deleted within 30 days. So deleted data disappears from all backups within 30 days.
  • Usage data is kept only as long as we need it for the purposes above. Turning off Share usage analytics deletes that device's usage data from our analytics server.
  • Server logs are rotated automatically and kept for a limited time.
  • Usage data is kept for up to 13 months and then deleted. Turning off Share usage analytics, or deleting your account, deletes what your device has sent right away.
  • Providers that only process requests (the AI and speech providers) keep data according to their own policies.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your exam and practice records, review plan, checkride date, knowledge test codes, disputes and follow-ups, and revokes the app's Sign in with Apple access. The usage data from the device you delete it on is deleted from our analytics server too. It can't be undone.

We keep the device ID and the count of free sessions started on that device, without any link to your account, so that deleting and re-creating an account doesn't restore free sessions. The two DeviceCheck flags Apple keeps for the device also stay.

Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel it in your iPhone's Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.

You can also email zhangdagen@xfrongchuang.com to ask us to access, correct, or delete your data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, California and other US states with privacy laws), you may have the right to know what we've collected, get a copy, correct it, delete it, and not be discriminated against for using these rights. Email zhangdagen@xfrongchuang.com. We'll verify the request comes from the account holder and answer within the time the law requires.

Children

TrueDPE is meant for student pilots preparing for a checkride. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app, contact us and we'll delete the account.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to the server and database is limited to people who run the service. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we'll update the effective date and let you know in the app before the change applies.
