Common questions

The examiner can't hear me. Open the iPhone Settings app, find TrueDPE, and turn on Microphone and Speech Recognition. You can also type your answers: tap Type next to the talk button.

I bought a plan but the app still shows the free limit. Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account that made the purchase.

How do I cancel the monthly subscription? Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose TrueDPE and tap Cancel Subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for. The one-time purchase doesn't renew and has nothing to cancel.

How do I get a refund? Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I think a grade is wrong. On the debrief, open the item and tap I disagree. The answer is graded again against the FAA source text and the result is shown to you. AI-generated feedback can be wrong; the FAA references and your instructor are the final word.

Where do my answers go? Your voice is turned into text on your iPhone and the audio never leaves it. The text of your answers is sent to our server and to an AI provider for grading. The app asks for your permission first, and you can withdraw it in Settings → AI data sharing. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account and data? In the app, open Settings → Delete account. This removes your account, exam history and review plan from our server. Backups are cleared within 30 days, as the Privacy Policy explains.

About

TrueDPE is a study aid for the FAA Private Pilot (Airplane) practical test oral exam. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the FAA, and the AI examiner is not a Designated Pilot Examiner.
