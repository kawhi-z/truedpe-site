1. What TrueDPE is — and isn't

TrueDPE is a study and practice tool for the FAA Private Pilot (Airplane Single-Engine Land) oral exam. It simulates an examiner, grades your answers against the Airman Certification Standards, and suggests what to review.

  • It is not an FAA exam, and a result in the app is not an FAA result. Passing a mock checkride doesn't mean you'll pass the real one.
  • It is not flight instruction and doesn't replace your certificated flight instructor (CFI), your instructor's endorsement, or the official FAA publications (14 CFR, the AIM, the ACS, FAA handbooks, and your aircraft's POH/AFM).
  • The examiner and the grading use AI. AI can be wrong — it may misgrade an answer, misstate a rule, or use outdated information. The FAA references and your instructor are the final word. Never rely on the app for a real flight decision.

We're not affiliated with or endorsed by the FAA.
